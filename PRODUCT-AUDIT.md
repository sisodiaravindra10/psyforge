# PSYFORGE — Product Improvement Plan

## Verdict

PSYFORGE's engine is the strong part and it is stronger than any of the six lenses initially credited: 12 synth voices across 4 bass and 8 lead topologies, per-preset kick/clap/duck tuning, a tempo-synced ping-pong send bus with 13 calibrated send points, per-step ratchets and probability, per-bar chain transpose, macro automation that replays through the same `_trigger` path as the offline WAV renderer, and 18 presets that measurably differ (Neuro Roller vs Yano Groove: centroid 1119 vs 565 Hz, 2.3 vs 5.5 transients/bar). The offline export is a genuine architectural win — `export.js` rebuilds the same graph, so most engine work reaches the WAV for free.

Two structural things hold it back. First, **nothing persists and nothing is undoable**: zero `localStorage`, zero `location.hash`, zero undo, and `applyPreset` (`js/ui.js:742-751`) overwrites all four slots plus the chain on a bare `change` event. A tool you cannot save, reopen, or hand to someone is a session toy no matter how good the engine is. Second, **the exposed surface is a fraction of the engine**: five macros and a preset dropdown gate 12 voices, so the only route to a different timbre is a dropdown that destroys your pattern — which is why four separate lenses independently reported "hidden depth" findings.

The third, smaller structural issue is a real live-vs-export and live-vs-mixer divergence cluster: per-track mute and fader do not cut that track's delay send, BUILD cannot reach a WAV at all, and DRIVE automation collapses to one constant value offline. Those are correctness bugs, not taste.

## The big rocks

### 1. Persistence, undo, and a share link — one serializer, three consumers
**Converged lenses:** firstrun ("preset change destroys everything"), engineering ("zero persistence"), stickiness ("no URL-hash state", "nothing survives a reload"). Four findings, one gap.

Today: `grep localStorage|sessionStorage|indexedDB|location.hash|history\.|undo|confirm(` over `js/` + `index.html` → **zero hits**. `applyPreset` (`js/ui.js:742-751`) sets `currentSlot='A'`, overwrites A via `loadTracks`, regenerates B/C/D via `makeVariations`, and hard-replaces `seq.chain` with `['B','B','A','A','D','D','D','D','C','C','D','D']` — fired from a bare `change` handler at `:881`, and since `:817` bails on `SELECT`, arrow-keying a focused dropdown wipes four slots with no click at all.

Build, in this order:

1. **Undo at the intent layer** (~30 lines). `pushUndo()` = `structuredClone({patterns, chain, mode, rootMidi, scale, bpm})`, called from the ~12 discrete intent handlers: grid `mousedown` (`:240`, once per gesture — never from the `mouseenter` paint path), `contextmenu`/`wheel`, `gen-euclid` (`:373`), `gen-dice` (`:381`), `auto-clr` (`:402`), `dup` (`:261`), `chainInsert`/`chainMove`/`chain CLR`/`+A-D`, `applyPreset`. **Explicitly exclude** `selectSlot`, `buildGrid`, and the live-record paths (`:425-426, :505, :712`). Do **not** snapshot from `renderCell`/`renderAll`/`renderChain`: `renderAll` is 144 `renderCell` calls on a slot-tab click, `mouseenter` paint fires one per cell, and automation writes (`:425-426`) and `CLR AUTO` (`:402-405`) call no render function at all — so the render layer both over-fires and misses the hardest edit to redo by hand. Coalesce at 300 ms, bind ⌘Z / ⌘⇧Z.
2. **Non-blocking preset-load undo.** In `applyPreset`, stash before touching anything; after applying, show a "PRESET LOADED · UNDO" chip beside the dropdown for ~10 s. **Never `window.confirm`** — `sequencer._tick` is a 25 ms `setInterval` with only 120 ms of runway (`js/sequencer.js:29`), so a modal starves the audio graph mid-jam. And the naive dirty check ("any slot differs") is true after one grid click and always true for the machine-generated B/C/D, so it would fire on essentially every preset change in an 18-preset audition app.
3. **One serializer, `pagehide` + 10 s idle → `localStorage['psyforge.v1']`.** `{v:1, presetId, bpm, rootMidi, scale, swing, mode, currentSlot, chain, patterns (incl. auto), params: engine.params, levels, mutes, viz:{styleIndex, word}}`. Measured 27 KB clean / 34 KB with automation on all four slots, ~26 µs per `stringify` — no bit-packer needed. Wrap in try/catch for private-mode quota.
4. **Restore explicitly, never silently.** Keep `applyPreset('fullon')` at `js/ui.js:1052` so first-run and demo visitors always get the curated hero, then show a "↺ RESTORE LAST SESSION" chip. Extract the DOM-sync block from `applyPreset` (`:773-788` plus the `.track-vol` loop) into `syncControls()` that reads from `engine.params`/`engine.levels`/`seq` rather than from the preset object — otherwise the five macro sliders, swing, bpm, root, scale and per-track volumes silently disagree with the restored engine.
5. **Share link, same serializer.** `JSON.stringify` → `CompressionStream('deflate-raw')` → base64url → `#1.<payload>`. Measured **381 B deflated / ~508 URL chars** for a real 4-slot state (`JSON.stringify` = 26.6 KB). No hand-packed bitfield — it buys ~300 URL characters nobody counts in exchange for a version-fragile fixed-order header, and collapsing `vel` to one accent bit loses the 0.95 written by VOX REC (`:502`) and FRED grid-rec (`:710`). Decode is async, so gate the first `requestAnimationFrame` on a ready flag. COPY LINK next to the WAV button (`index.html:75`) + `history.replaceState`.

**Handle the FRED buffer explicitly.** `engine.voiceBuffer` (`js/audio-engine.js:40`) is a live `AudioBuffer` that cannot go in a URL or in a ~5 MB localStorage quota (10 s mono 44.1k = 1.76 MB Float32). Set `needsVoice: true` when any slot has an `on` step on the `voice` track and show a one-line banner ("this beat uses a voice note — record your own in FRED MODE"); `voice()` early-returns at `:892`, so silent restore produces a lit-but-dead row. For a downloadable project file later, embed the take as a WAV chunk using the encoder already in `js/export.js`, and capture `new Blob(chunks)` **before** `decodeAudioData` at `js/ui.js:623` (decode detaches the ArrayBuffer). Do not persist a recording of the user's real voice across sessions without an explicit opt-in.

**Effort:** M (undo S, autosave S, hash M). **Unlocks:** the app stops being a session toy; a link is a save file; remix chains; and the same snapshot feeds a future named-slots library. This is the single highest-leverage change in the plan.

### 2. Expose the voices — 12 synth topologies behind one destructive dropdown
**Converged lenses:** producer (twice), stickiness. All three verifiers cut the "32 unreachable timbres" headline — every one of the 12 voices ships in at least one of the 18 presets, and `bassStyle` covers roll/reese/log/reverse while `leadStyle` covers all 8. What is genuinely missing is **orthogonal recombination**: my pattern + that timbre. `grep -n "leadStyle\|bassStyle\|kickTune\|kickAttack\|clapTone\|duckDepth" index.html js/ui.js` → **0 hits**; the entire surface is five sliders (`index.html:163-184`, `MACRO_MAP` at `js/ui.js:409-415`).

Build:

1. **Two `<select>`s** in the BASS and LEAD strips (or SOUND DESIGN): `bassStyle` = roll/reese/log/reverse, `leadStyle` = acid/saws/fm/pluck/chord/tumbi/cowbell/screech, bound with `engine.setParam(...)`. Both params are read at trigger time (`js/audio-engine.js:343, 546`) and `js/export.js:21` deep-copies `engine.params`, so the WAV inherits them with zero engine changes. Add them to the preset-sync block at `js/ui.js:776-780` so a preset load repaints them.
2. **Pair the decay with the voice.** A bare select ships a broken groove: measured, `roll → reese` on a psy 16th roll takes duty 35% → 75% and crest 9.7 → 3.5 dB, because `_bassReese`/`_bassLog` floor their gate at `max(0.15, bassDecay*2.4)`/`max(0.18, bassDecay*2.2)` — longer than a 103 ms 16th — and every shipped preset pairs those styles with `bassDecay ≈ 0.3`, not the psy 0.055. On change, snap `bassDecay` (roll ~55 ms, others ~300 ms) and move the `m-decay` slider. Same for `leadStyle` ↔ `leadRes` (acid ~12-14, chord/pluck ~2-3).
3. **Three new numeric macros, added to `MACRO_MAP`** so they inherit AUTO record/replay for free (`_applyAuto` is param-name-generic): **PUMP** (`duckDepth`, inverted so right = deeper — the most recognizable EDM mechanic and currently invisible), **KICK CHARACTER** (`kickAttack`, real preset range 240–2200, not the comment's 350–1700), optionally **CLAP TONE** (`clapTone`; measured clap centroid spans 492 Hz at 1150 to 2605 Hz at 2000 — a 5× timbral spread already authored in 6 presets).
4. **Keep the two selects out of `MACRO_MAP`.** `bindMacro` computes `+e.target.value * scale` → number → `pattern.auto` → replayed by both the live scheduler and `export.js:44`; a string yields NaN in the pattern and poisons the render. And `js/sequencer.js:267` branches on `leadStyle === 'chord'` to stack a triad, so per-step automation of it would silently re-harmonize the lead mid-bar.

**Also:** `chordVoicing` as a single engine param extending the existing degree stack at `js/sequencer.js:267-271` — triad `[d,d+2,d+4]`, 7th `[d,d+2,d+4,d+6]`, sus `[d,d+3,d+4]`, default `'triad'` so all 18 presets sound identical. Because the stack is already in scale degrees through `PSY.degreeToSemis`, `d+6` is the correct diatonic 7th in every scale. ~5 lines.

**Effort:** S (selects + decay pairing) + S (three macros). **Unlocks:** removes the reason to reload a preset mid-session, which is most of the destructive-dropdown complaint; turns 18 presets into a sound library; makes PUMP automatable.

### 3. Fix the live↔export and live↔mixer divergences
**Converged lenses:** sound, producer, engineering. Defects 1, 2 and 3 above are one theme: the offline renderer is 95% faithful and the remaining 5% is invisible to the user. Ship them together and state the contract explicitly in the ABOUT panel: what you hear is what you render, except TTS (already labelled at `index.html:156`).

Two things to nail while in there:
- **Raise `tail` in `js/export.js:15` from 2 s to 3.5–4 s** if you add any reverb or lengthen the delay, and drop the word "reverb" from that comment now (it describes a node that does not exist).
- **Render-to-render variance is not the probability roll.** Two exports of an untouched preset are already byte-different at 100% probability; the dominant term is `js/audio-engine.js:139-141`, where each `Engine` fills an 88,200-sample shared noise buffer from `Math.random()`, and `export.js:22` builds a new Engine. The delta is −24 dB relative — the instrument's intentional character. If bit-reproducibility is ever wanted, seed *that* buffer; leave `each()` (`js/sequencer.js:237`) alone, since per-step probability is a humanize feature the user opted into per step.

**Effort:** M for all three defects. **Unlocks:** the mixer becomes trustworthy (mute actually mutes), the drop survives export, and macro automation is uniformly faithful.

### 4. Sound: gain-staging and hat presence before any new DSP
**Converged lenses:** sound (twice), producer (twice). The verifiers demolished the "no 2–6 kHz anywhere / no reverb / functionally mono" framing but left a real, cheap tuning pass.

Do in this order:

1. **Preset gain-staging — measurably the dominant term, zero code.** In `js/presets.js` raise `chat` 0.4 → 0.55-0.6 and `ohat` 0.45 → 0.6-0.65, and pull `kick` 0.95 → ~0.80 and `bass` 0.9 → ~0.75 so the −12 dB/5:1 master comp is not already saturated. Measured with no engine edit: fullon 8k octave −23.1 → **−10.8 dB** rel 63 Hz, 16k −24.4 → −12.0; Goa Acid −8.5/−9.5; Insane −11.8. That lands inside the released-master window the "presence cliff" finding cited as the target. Also fix the two genuinely odd kick tunings where kick and bass actually co-trigger: garba (D1 root, `kickTune` 57 = +7.62 st → try 55 or 45) and possibly amapiano (45 vs A1). **Do not** couple `kickTune` to `rootMidi` — it regresses SONG-mode per-bar transpose (±12 st would make the kick a 90 Hz tom) and the D1–A1 root selector would swing every kick 36.7–55 Hz.
2. **Widen the hats in place** (`js/audio-engine.js:514-537`, ~8 lines, still 3-4 nodes/hit): add `hatTone`/`hatDecay` to `Engine.DEFAULTS` and read them instead of the hardcoded 7900/6800 and 0.045/0.18. Closed HP 7900 → ~5200, open 6800 → ~4800; one peaking biquad at 3.5-4.5 kHz (+4 dB, Q 0.8) so the 2.8-5.6 kHz octave stops being a hole (it is the thinnest band even with hats maxed, −18.7 dB); closed decay → ~0.065 s, open → ~0.23 s; map the already-passed `vel` onto HP frequency for accent brightness. Then set the new keys per preset — short bright shaker for Yano Groove and Raga Garba, tighter tick for Neuro Roller, longer open hat for Full-On. Defaults preserve today's sound for any preset that omits them.
3. **Kick body plateau — one line.** Between `js/audio-engine.js:215` and `:216`, hold before decaying so the sweep lands while there is still amplitude (currently −15.1 dB by the 45 ms landing): hoist `const swp = start > 800 ? 0.045 : 0.03;` (reuse at `:213`), then `setValueAtTime(1.05*vel, t)` → `exponentialRampToValueAtTime(0.75*vel, t+swp)` → `exponentialRampToValueAtTime(0.0001, t+dec)`. Measured through the real master chain: fullon downbeat <90 Hz −13.6 → **−4.0 dB**, master peak unchanged at −0.86, psy click preserved (kick-solo mid band 26.9% vs choir 4.9%), and choir's sidechain duck gap essentially untouched (−13.2 → −12.0 dB, vs −6.4 under a two-layer rewrite). Gate to `0.55` for `start > 800` if it reads too boomy for full-on.
4. **Width from the wet path only.** `delayIn` feeds `delayL` and `delayR` gets nothing but L's feedback (`js/audio-engine.js:123-133`) — it is not a true ping-pong. Feed `delayIn` into `delayR` as well with a 2-4 ms (sub-Haas) tap offset. ## Deeper bets

**Stems export.** Once the post-fader send gains exist (defect 1), stems are nearly free: N sequential `PSY.exportWav` passes with all mutes true except one, since `export.js:23` already copies mutes offline. Two non-obvious requirements the naive version gets wrong: keep `comp` and `clip` **in** on every pass (bypassing them means the stems sum to the pre-master-chain mix, not to the WAV the user has), and render one extra "delay return" stem with all nine track gains at 0 and `delayIn` live so the N+1 files sum bit-exactly (measured −155 dB residual once the shared return is separated; with the leak present the residual is 33-124% of mix RMS and peaks overshoot 3.3×). Ship as N+1 download buttons, not a hand-rolled store-mode ZIP with a CRC32 table. **Tradeoff:** narrow audience — only people finishing a track around PSYFORGE's own voices — and it depends on defect 1 landing first.

**MIDI export.** Every conversion is already pure data: `js/sequencer.js:252` (bass), `:269-271` (chord triad), `:274` (lead), `:283` (vox), `:91-113` (chain transpose), `:236-243` (ratchet/prob). ~120 lines of type-1 SMF, no dependencies. **Tradeoff, stated honestly:** the payload is thinner than it sounds — five psy presets ship `tk(ROLL, [...])` with no note map, so their bass exports as twelve identical root notes, and no preset sets `ratchet` or `prob` at all. Three things live inside pattern data that MIDI has no home for: `pat.auto` (emit as CC74/71/91/93 rather than dropping it), `pat.vox[i].vowel` (track-name text events), and the `voice` track, whose `note` is a slice index into the FRED buffer, not a pitch. Also: "bake probability at 100% *or* roll once to match what was heard" is unsatisfiable — `each()` rolls live and the offline render re-rolls, so force `prob = 1` and say so in the UI. And swung note-ons need the preceding note trimmed or the monophonic bass/lead overlap on import.

**A SPACE (reverb) send bus.** Only worth it *after* the free width work in rock 4. `ConvolverNode` (not an FDN — Web Audio has no allpass primitive, and a Schroeder network is 80+ nodes of by-ear tuning) plus a ~30-line procedural IR generator, reusing the same `createBuffer` + `Math.random` pattern as `this.noiseBuf` at `js/audio-engine.js:133`. Feed it from the per-voice send gains that already exist (`:328, 553, 606, 644, 683, 715, 770, 913, 949`) and the existing 250 Hz `delayHP`, not a new set. Because it lives in `init(ctx)`, the export path inherits it with no change to `exportWav`. Cap the IR at 1.6 s or raise `tail` to 3.5 — a 2.2 s hall does not fit `tail = 2`. Ship **one** IR and one SPACE macro (which inherits AUTO for free), not two. Kick and bass send 0. **Tradeoff:** measured against the free option, moving three presets' `delayMix` plus raising the slider ceiling already delivers more width (side/mid −16.6 → −11.6 dB, corr 0.9576 → 0.8801) at zero code. Only build the convolver if you specifically want a *diffuse, non-rhythmic* tail.

**Video clip capture.** Technically sound and the graph is ready (`clip → analyser → destination` at `js/audio-engine.js:81-82` is a clean tap; GL is `alpha:false` and the 2D layer is a true `destination-out` overlay, so same-rAF `drawImage` composites without `preserveDrawingBuffer`). Three things must be right or the file is garbage: size the compositor **once** to a fixed user-chosen target (1080×1080 / 1920×1080 / 1080×1920), never from `getBoundingClientRect` — `.viz-wrap` is 340px, `100vh` in `:fullscreen`, 220px mobile, and a mid-stream frame-size change kills mp4 output; **pin `this.quality`** and short-circuit the demotion at `js/visualizer.js:590-598`, or recording trips the adaptive downgrade and the clip comes out worse than the preview; and gate the compositor `drawImage` behind `if (this.recording)` so the unconditional `viz.frame()` at `js/ui.js:1040` pays nothing. **Tradeoff:** re-framing is above M because `_draw2D` derives W/H/cx/cy/minDim/shake from `this.canvas.width` (`:652-654, :681`), and the honest baseline is that fullscreen viz plus any OS screen recorder already covers this.

**Per-slot pattern length / per-track subdivision.** Genuinely a rearchitecture, and the sequencing case for it is weaker than it looks — the chain loops (`chainEntry` returns `chain[bar % chain.length]`), swing goes to 40% (past triplet swing at 66:34, and four presets already use it as their genre feel), and ratchet ×3 on a shaker step *is* 16th-triplets and renders offline through the same `_trigger`. Changing the 16-step clock touches the live scheduler, `js/export.js:41-46`, the 16-slot `pat.auto[param][i]` format, the VOX/FRED quantizers (`((k%16)+16)%16`), `PSY.euclid(hits, 16, rot)`, the 16-cell DOM grid, and all 18 presets' 16-length arrays. **Tradeoff:** L/XL for subdivisions that already exist by other means. The chain repeat count and the export bar-count input (both in Quick wins) capture ~90% of the arrangement pain for two small diffs.

## Do not build

- **Any "master fader is a drive control / kick pumps the mix" rewrite.** The topology claim is true but the numbers aren't: level-matched 0.85 vs 0.40 differs by ≤0.69 dB in every octave band, HF per-16th peak varies only 1.6 dB across the bar, and the *least* reduction falls on the kick step (the 3 ms attack passes the hat transient). Hats never enter the duck bus. Do the ~5-line post-clipper `outTrim` in rock 4 instead; do not remove the kick from the comp and do not add comp amount/bypass UI.
- **Hard-panning detuned oscillator pairs at ±0.7.** Measured cost: lead RMS −4.02 dB (fullon), −3.48 (bigroom), because destroying the coherent summing of two ±9-cent saws removes the thickness *and* 2-4 dB of level; full-mix effect ranges −3.7 to +1.4 dB across 13 presets that would all need re-auditioning. It is a no-op for insane/goa/forest/hardstyle/dnb (single-oscillator leads, empty vox rows), it decenters sub on reese/log, and splitting vox requires duplicating the whole 3-bandpass formant bank on the voice the VOX pad fires 8 at a time. Never put an 11 ms Haas offset on a dry voice — the WAV is the deliverable and it comb-filters in mono.
- **A 10th percussion track, or splitting `clap()` into clap/snare/rim modes.** The track ships empty in all 18 presets (so it changes no kit's sound until every preset is re-authored) while touching `TRACKS`, `blankPattern`, `copyPattern`, grid rows, arrange drag-drop, the euclid target list, viz event chips, level/mute rows and CSS row heights. The clap split retroactively changes 18 shipped presets and every previously exported WAV reference. The escape hatch already ships: FRED MODE accepts a dropped audio file, slices it onto 8 pads with ±12 st, and sequences it.
- **The 6-oscillator metal bank at ratios × 205 Hz into BP 8400.** Measured: peaks at 5050 Hz with 19.5% of its power below 2 kHz (mud under the bass) and only 41% in 6-12k, at 15-18 nodes per hit on the busiest voice (9.7 closed hits/s). It only behaves as spec'd at Q≥4 or with a base near 800 Hz. The 808 metal topology also already exists — `leadStyle: 'cowbell'` at `js/audio-engine.js:586-608`, sequenceable with per-step pitch.
- **A 24-28 Hz highpass on the bass or master, or a parallel undecayed sine sub.** Measured: the HP frees **0.00 dB** of peak (−0.85 → −0.86, identical with comp+clip bypassed) while costing 0.6 LU on fullon and 2.25 LU on dnb and 1.6 dB in the 60-250 Hz band. The "DC offset" is not DC — the silent tail measures 3.64e-7; it is the AC asymmetry of a legitimate 41 Hz pluck. A master HP would also eat the kick's designed 30 Hz sweep floor (kick-only "DC" is −70.5 dBFS, 70× below the bass). The undecayed sub destroys the amplitude gap that makes the roll read as a roll and that `duckAt` pumps against.
- **Raising the roll bass's filter floor from 100 to 250-400 Hz.** Measured >600 Hz share: 0.72% → 0.97%. The amp envelope is ~42 dB down by `fallTime`, so the floor value is inaudible. Close the 2-6 kHz gap in the hats/clap/lead, not the sub bass.
- **A seeded PRNG in `each()`/`hat()` for reproducible exports.** Applied verbatim, two renders are still byte-different (fullon `3c8debd4…` vs `7a9fb095…`); the dominant randomness is the per-Engine noise buffer at `js/audio-engine.js:139-141`, which the proposal never mentions. Two exports of an untouched all-100%-probability preset are *already* byte-different, so probability is not the cause. Zero of 598 active steps across all 18 presets ship `prob < 1`.
- **A new automation architecture ("16 stepped values that reset every bar").** Auto lanes live per *slot* and `_applyAuto` is fed `patternForBar(bar)`, so a 4-bar chain expresses 64 distinct points — measured on a real export: 63 sounding notes over 8.72 s with brightness climbing 44 → 644 monotonically across the chain. The continuous into-the-drop sweep is already `Engine.riser` (`:836-859`), a sample-accurate 350 → 4800 Hz ramp on a labelled button. And restructuring bass/lead filters as long-lived shared nodes destroys the per-note velocity-dependent filter envelope that *is* the psy bass (`peak = clamp(bassCutoff * (0.6 + 0.55*vel))` then ramped to 100 Hz per note, with notes overlapping 0.48 s gate against a 0.10 s step).
- **A sidechain-duck curve rewrite.** The proposed `linearRampToValueAtTime` without a `setValueAtTime` anchor never returns to 1.0 — measured envelope locks at ~0.44-0.50 for a whole bar, so bass/lead/vox/voice/fx/delay would sit a permanent −6 to −7 dB down with the pump gone, live *and* in every WAV. The HF-splatter evidence doesn't reproduce (a 4 ms attack moves 8-16 kHz by ±0.14 dB and makes it *worse* in two of three presets), and in 2 of 3 presets a no-duck control shows the duck step isn't even the peak-slew source. `duckDepth` also has no UI to widen, and presets already ship 0.2 = −14 dB.
- **Making `transposeForBar` diatonic, or snapping the melody to the nearest in-scale tone.** Chords are *already* resolved diatonically — `[deg, deg+2, deg+4]` are scale degrees through `PSY.degreeToSemis`, so Festival Choir emits I-I-vi-IV-V in key, and scrolling degrees 0,5,2,6 in E Phrygian gives i-VI-III-VII. Changing the semantics breaks the shipped scroll-a-block-to-transpose feature, the `{s,t}`/legacy-string chain format, and the offline renderer, and would silently rewrite every chained psy/goa riff where chromatic whole-riff transposition is deliberate.
- **`touch-action: none` on `.cell`**, a **play-gate overlay / frozen first frame** (`.viz-overlay` hosts the live WORD input, style and fullscreen buttons; freezing `rot` kills the kinetic-word wobble that works fine with zero audio, and a dead canvas already means VIZ OFF), **144 `tabindex="0"` gridcells** (needs the global keydown handler rewritten first, and buries every other control behind 144 tab stops), a **350 ms click-and-hold on the grid** as specified (mousedown already toggles *and* arms `paint`, so it either fires on a step you just flipped or adds latency to the most-used action), a **hover-only gesture strip** (deletes the only documentation of space/B/V/1-4 and the FRED file drop, and is zero-discoverability on touch), **renaming VOX→CHOIR / VOICE→FRED** (collides with the "Festival Choir" preset and contradicts `index.html:140`'s own "VOICE row" copy), **broadening the Space guard to `button`** (kills Space/b/v/1-4/q-i whenever focus parks on a button after a mouse click), and a **`window.confirm` gate on preset load** (blocks `_tick`, starving the audio graph in ~120 ms).
- **A date-seeded "daily forge".** The euclid generator is already deterministic (`PSY.euclid` is pure integer math — two people typing hits=5 rot=2 get identical rhythms), `makeVariations` is deterministic, and the 18 presets are already 18 shared reproducible starting states with artist attribution. Seeding the dice buys nothing while step probability still rolls live in `_trigger`, and replacing the curated `fullon` boot with a dice roll decouples the ABOUT text from what's playing.
